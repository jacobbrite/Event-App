Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

BriteWing Events ("we", "us") is a service for organizing gatherings, such as book clubs, and inviting guests to them. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and who can see it. The service is currently in beta.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. Your name and email address, and a password if you sign up with email. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored in hashed form; we never see or store your password in plain text.
  • Google sign-in. If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We request only basic profile and email access. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data.
  • Profile photo. A picture you upload, or the one that comes from your Google account. You can change or replace it on your profile page.
  • Event activity. Events you create (title, date and time, location, description), invitations, and your RSVPs, including whether you are going or have cancelled.
  • Technical information. Your browser stores a session token so you stay signed in. Our hosting providers may log basic technical details such as IP address and browser type when you use the site.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and the events you host or are invited to.
  • To send emails about your account and the events you're part of, such as account confirmation and password reset.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show advertising.

3. Who can see your information

  • Event hosts. The host of an event you are invited to can see your name, email address and RSVP status for that event.
  • Event details. Guests who are invited to an event can see that event's details. Guests cannot see the guest list or each other's email addresses.
  • Other members. Features that involve other members of a group (for example, book suggestions or votes) may show your name and photo to those members.
  • Profile photos. Photos are stored in a location that can be opened by anyone who has the image's link. They are not listed or searchable on the site, but you should not upload a picture you would not want others to be able to view.
  • Us. The people who operate the service can access stored data as needed to run and support it.

4. Service providers

We use these companies to run the service. They process information on our behalf and only as needed to provide their services:

  • Supabase for our database, authentication and file storage.
  • Vercel for hosting the website.
  • Resend for delivering emails.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers.

6. How long we keep information, and deleting it

We keep your information while your account is active. You can edit your name and photo on your profile page at any time. To delete your account and the associated information, email us at the address below and we will do so. Copies may remain in backups for a limited time before they are overwritten.

7. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will respect them where they apply.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including database-level access controls that limit who can read each record. No method of storing or transmitting data is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The service is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. When we do, we will change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will make a reasonable effort to let you know.
